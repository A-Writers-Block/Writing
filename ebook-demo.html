Money spider

I remember laying on my prison bunk once flat on my belly on top of my mattress laying there looking at the floor
my head was hanging off the end of the bed and my legs were dangling in the air I was just starring at the floor aimlessly I lost track of time I could of been of staring at the floor for hours for all I know and I remember a little spider crawling along the floor I remember thinking to myself I wonder where you are going little spider in such a hurry I just kept starring at him and watched him hurry towards the gap underneath the cell door and I realised he was also desperate to escape too
6
I could of stomped on that little spiders head and squashed him scraping his remains off my boot
killing all his dreams of freedom like mine were and crushing them into oblivion completely forgotten like my future which had become just shadows and dust
but I let him run free
and I knew right then one day my time would come too
and I would also run free
realising I still had one dream left
all I had to do was keep running
and pray no one stamped on my head
yesterday The Beetles
Dreams don't die people just stop dreaming

Isaiah

Isaiah
why should you be beaten anymore why do you
persist in rebellion
your whole head is injured your whole heart afflicted
from the sole of your foot to the top of your head there is no soundness only wounds and bruises and open sores not cleaned or bandaged.
you are desolate
and still you smoke that hell with your fire
lord I still have clean air in my lungs to pollute
8
I can still crawl on my belly or drag my blistered feet across town my body is tired I'm exhausted frail and seem weak my mind is distorted but there's still blood flowing in my veins to poison the fire in my belly is yet to ignite I find no shelter from your bitter wind relentless rain and to you I curse daily with my sharp tongue with such vicious words of hatred I wish to live not yet I'm too afraid to die as I know what awaits me in death you ask why I persist I'm too cowardly to take my own life so I continue to struggle in pain completely lost and destitute hoping one day I shall be found and given a soft bed to lay my crown and maybe even one day find the peace I once knew as a young child but until that day I shall persist and live each day to be beaten relentlessly as I only find comfort in pain
